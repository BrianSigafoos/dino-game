# Dino Game

## Concept

A playful dino-learning game where friendly dinosaurs guide kids through simple
facts and skills using flash cards. Each card asks a short dino-themed question,
and the player answers by either writing the answer or choosing right/wrong. The
game celebrates the pun: "write or wrong" decisions are part of the fun.

## Target Audience

- Ages 4-8
- Short sessions (3-10 minutes)
- Low reading level, big visuals, simple choices

## Core Loop

1. Draw a flash card with a dino host.
2. Player responds (write, tap, or choose right/wrong).
3. Dino reacts with encouragement and a small reward.
4. Progress along a mini adventure path.

## Flash Card Types

- Dino facts: "Is a Triceratops a plant eater?"
- Body parts: "Point to the tail."
- Habitats: "Do dinos live in the ocean?"
- Time basics: "Did dinos live before humans?"
- Counting: "How many eggs are in the nest?"
- Letters: "Which letter does 'Raptor' start with?"
- Shapes/Colors: "Tap the green footprint."
- Simple words: "Write the word 'DINO'."

## Write or Wrong Options

- Write Mode: trace or free-write letters/numbers.
- Right/Wrong Mode: choose yes/no for true-or-false cards.
- Mixed Mode: randomize for variety.

## Gameplay Options

- Dino Trail Adventure: move a dino token along a path, one card per step.
- Egg Rescue: answer cards to collect eggs before time runs out.
- Volcano Dash: speed round where correct answers cool the volcano.
- Fossil Builder: correct cards unlock fossil pieces to assemble.
- Dino Care: answer cards to feed, groom, or decorate a pet dino.
- Museum Tour: answer cards to unlock new exhibit rooms and facts.

## Rewards and Motivation

- Sticker collection for each dino.
- Simple badges (Rookie, Explorer, Paleontologist).
- Unlock new dino friends and habitats.

## Technical Notes

- Web-only game using plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Runs as a simple GitHub Pages app (no backend).
- Works on desktop and tablet browsers.
